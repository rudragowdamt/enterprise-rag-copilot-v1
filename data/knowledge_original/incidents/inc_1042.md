# Incident
Payment authorization failed with repeated HTTP 401 responses.
# Root cause
The client certificate used for service authentication had expired after certificate rotation was missed.
# Resolution
The operations team installed the renewed certificate through the approved secret process, restarted the affected application instances in a controlled manner, and validated payment authorization.
# Learning
Add certificate-expiry alerting at 30, 14 and 7 days before expiry.
