title: Production P1 Incident Escalation Policy
doc_type: policy
system: EnterpriseOperations
environment: PROD

A P1 is declared for widespread loss of a critical production business service, severe customer impact, or material transaction failure. The incident manager must establish the major-incident bridge, engage the application owner and required dependency teams, maintain an incident timeline, and issue stakeholder updates at the defined major-incident cadence. Recovery actions must be documented and followed by RCA.
