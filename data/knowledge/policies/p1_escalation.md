# P1 Major Incident Escalation Policy
Document ID: POL-P1-001

Declare P1 when a critical production service has severe business impact, widespread transaction failure or no acceptable workaround. Start the major-incident bridge, assign technical and incident leads, preserve correlation IDs/log evidence, engage required platform/dependency owners, communicate at agreed intervals and maintain a timeline. Restoration is prioritized over speculative root-cause changes; RCA follows stabilization.
